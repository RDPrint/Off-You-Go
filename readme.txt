Off You Go
==========

A tiny, no-nonsense Windows shutdown timer.

What it does
------------
Lets you schedule your PC to Shutdown, Restart, or Log Off either:
- At a specific time of day (e.g., 11:30 PM), or
- After a set amount of time (days, hours, and minutes).

Once you click Schedule, the timer is handed off directly to Windows (shutdown.exe). You can close the app and the shutdown will still happen on time. If you change your mind, just open the app and click "Cancel Shutdown" to call it off.

How to use
----------
1. Run OffYouGo.exe.
2. Pick your action (Shutdown, Restart, or Log Off) and when you want it to happen.
3. Click Schedule.
4. Close the app whenever you like - or leave it open to see the countdown in the bottom bar.

Building from source
--------------------
All source code is inside the "src" folder. No heavy tools or installs needed - just double-click build.bat and it will use the C# compiler built into Windows to create OffYouGo.exe.
